About

My name is Simon Peterson, and this bakery is my chance to finally fulfill my lifelong dream.

My love for baking started when I was a very young boy, making pastries in the kitchen with my mother. I have loved baking ever since and have made far too many pastries and cakes to count.

I have dreamed of opening my own bakery for a long time, and now, after retiring, I finally have the time and energy to make that dream come true. Most of my products are based on old recipes from my late mother that I have kept throughout the years.

I hope you will stop by my bakery and support an old man who is finally living his dream.
